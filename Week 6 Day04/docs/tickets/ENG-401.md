# ENG-401 · Agreement between two raters

**Type:** feature · **Priority:** Must · **Day 4, Activity 2**

## Story

As the FDE, I want to measure how far my labels, and later an LLM judge's verdicts, agree with Tom's, so that I know whether our evaluation measures what the client means by "right", and not what I happen to think.

## Acceptance criteria

1. `agreement(rater, expert, positive)` in `backend/app/evaluation/metrics.py` returns an `Agreement` exactly as its docstring describes:
   - **n** counts only items both raters labelled; **percent** is plain agreement
   - **kappa** is Cohen's kappa: agreement beyond what chance would give, `(po − pe) / (1 − pe)`
   - **tp, fp, fn, tn** with the rater in the rows and the expert in the columns; `positive` names the "problem" label
   - **caught** = tp / (tp + fn) and **cleared** = tn / (tn + fp), or `None` when there is nothing to divide by
   - **disagreements** lists the items where they differ, sorted
2. On **Evaluation → Labelling with Tom**, **Compare with Tom** shows the agreement, the kappa and the 2 × 2 table, and marks every item where you and Tom differ.
3. On **Evaluation → LLM judges**, **Run judge** shows caught, cleared and kappa for the judge against Tom's tags.
