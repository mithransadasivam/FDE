# ENG-301 · Ground every field on the words of the page

**Type:** feature · **Priority:** Must · **Day 3, Activity 2 · session "extract"** · **Story:** S02

## Story

As an analyst, I want each field's confidence to come from the words it was read from, and any value the page does not show to be flagged, so that I know exactly what to trust. This is what the 2024 pilot could not do (ADR-001).

## Acceptance criteria

1. `ground_fields(reply, words, ok_at, check_at)` in `backend/app/prototype/grounding.py` returns one `Field` per name in `FIELDS`, in that order, exactly as its docstring describes:
   - **confidence** is the weakest cited word's confidence, rounded to 2 decimals
   - **status** comes from `status_for()`: ok at 0.85 or more, check from 0.60, doubtful below
   - **box** is the union of the cited words' boxes
   - a value that does not match its cited words (letters and digits only, any case) is **not_on_page**, with confidence 0 and the note `The page says "…"`
   - no usable words: **not_on_page**; no value: **missing**
2. On **Cases**, opening D03-014 shows the statement date as *not what the page says*, with the page's own value offered instead.

## Files you change

- `backend/app/prototype/grounding.py`: fill in `ground_fields()`. Nothing else.

`norm()`, `status_for()` and `union_box()` are in the same file (done). The shapes are in `backend/app/prototype/models.py`: the contract, do not change it.
