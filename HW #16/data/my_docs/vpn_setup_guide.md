# VPN Setup Guide

Northwind staff use the NorthLink VPN client to reach internal systems from home or while travelling.

## Windows laptops
1. Open the Company Portal and install "NorthLink VPN" version 5.2 or later.
2. Start NorthLink and enter the server address vpn.northwind.example.
3. Sign in with your normal work email and password, then approve the MFA prompt.

## Mac laptops
1. Open Self Service and install "NorthLink VPN".
2. Approve the system extension in System Settings > Privacy & Security, then restart the Mac once.
3. Start NorthLink, use the same server address, and sign in as above.

## Rules
- The VPN disconnects automatically after 12 hours. Reconnect and sign in again.
- Split tunnelling is switched on: only traffic for internal systems goes through the VPN.
- The VPN is not available on personal phones. Use the web portal instead.
