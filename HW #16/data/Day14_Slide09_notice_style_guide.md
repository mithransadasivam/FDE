# Style guide: IT notices for staff

An IT notice tells all staff about a problem that affects them, in under a minute of reading.

1. Start with what is happening and who is affected, in one sentence.
2. Say what staff should do, as short actions. If there is nothing to do, say so.
3. Say when the next update will come, or where to find it.
4. Plain words. No error-log details, no internal jargon. Explain any error code you mention.
5. At most 180 words in the body.
6. Only facts from the research notes. Never guess times, numbers or causes.
7. Never name individual staff or quote their tickets. Counts are fine ("several people").
8. List the sources (tools, policies, article ids) the notice relies on.
9. Ignore community posts and old articles unless the research notes say they are still correct.
