*Everything in this brief is fictional: the bank, its people, its systems and its numbers.*

## What KYC onboarding means here

Before the bank opens an account, UK money-laundering rules require it to check who the customer is and where they live (Know Your Customer, KYC). Applicants upload documents in the app or at a branch:

- **Identity:** passport or driving licence
- **Proof of address:** a utility bill, bank statement or council tax bill
- **Business accounts only:** certificate of incorporation, the list of directors and the people with significant control

A KYC analyst opens each application in the case management system, reads every document, checks it against the application form and either approves it, asks the applicant for something else, or escalates it to the Financial Crime team.
