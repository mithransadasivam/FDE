**What we heard**
- Median of 7 working days against the 3 promised; 4,200 documents waiting.
- Business accounts: about 15% of applications, about 40% of analyst time.
- 31% of applications need an "ask applicant" request.
- The 2024 pilot stopped after 4 months because it never showed how sure it was.

**What your data adds**
Business accounts are 16.9% of applications but 46.9% of analyst minutes. Branches handle 24% of reviews but cause 58% of "proof of address older than 3 months" requests, so a date check at the counter may remove many of them, with no AI.

**What we propose to build first**
A proof-of-address assistant that highlights the doubtful part and shows how sure it is, while the analyst decides. Old bills are 28% of "ask applicant" requests, and it is a simple place to win back trust.
