# ENG-1102 · A field's confidence must say how likely it is to be right

**Type:** feature · **Priority:** Must · **Day 11, Activity 2** · **Story:** a smudged date is never trusted

## Story

As Deborah Okafor, Head of KYC Operations, I want the small model to say when it is unsure of a value, so that a misread date goes to the pilot or to an analyst instead of straight into CaseFlow.

## The problem today

In `backend/app/distil/reader.py`, `read_fields()` gives a field the **average** of the model's probabilities for its words. Two things are lost:

- the weakest word: a field is only as good as its weakest word, and an average hides it;
- the OCR's own confidence. On B-012, a scanned statement, the bank's OCR read a smudged "04" as "01" with confidence 0.61. The model sees "01" in the right place and is sure of it, so the date "01 Sep 2026" comes out at 0.88.

On **Model lab → Evaluate**, 14 of the small model's October errors have a confidence of 0.70 or more: all 14 are smudged dates.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A field's words are the words whose most likely label is the field, in reading order. Value: their texts joined with single spaces. `word_ids`: their ids.
2. confidence = the lowest, over the field's words, of (the model's probability for the field × the word's OCR confidence), rounded to 3 decimals.
3. A field with no words: value `""`, `word_ids` `[]`, confidence `0.0`.
4. On **Model lab → Read a document**, B-012's statement date "01 Sep 2026" has a confidence below 0.70. On **Evaluate**, no error has a confidence of 0.70 or more.

## Files you change
