# ENG-1103 · The cascade: the weakest field decides who reads the document

**Type:** feature · **Priority:** Must · **Day 11, Activity 3** · **Story:** the small model reads what it is sure of, the pilot reads the rest

## Story

As Helen Achebe, the MLRO, I want the small model's values to go straight through only when every field is sure, and every other document to be read by the pilot as today (ADR-003), so that the cascade lets no more errors through than the pilot alone.

## The problem today

In `backend/app/distil/cascade.py`, `route_document()` averages the four confidences. A document with a sure name, address and issuer and a smudged date at 0.52 averages 0.87 and goes straight through. On **Model lab → Cascade** at a threshold of 0.70, **14** errors slip through, against the pilot's 3.

## Acceptance criteria

1. `route_document(fields, threshold)` returns `route="small"` when every field has a value and a confidence of at least `threshold`, and `route="teacher"` otherwise.
2. `reasons`: one per field that sends the document to the teacher, in field order: `"statement_date missing"` when the value is empty, or `"statement_date 0.59 below 0.70"` (two decimals, the confidence rounded down: 0.699 is `"0.69 below 0.70"`).
3. On **Model lab → Cascade**, the October batch at 0.70: **no** small-model error slips through, and in all no more errors than the pilot's 3. About a fifth of the documents go to the pilot, plus the spot checks.

- `backend/app/distil/cascade.py`: `route_document()`. Keep `spot_check()`.
