# New Starter IT Checklist

## Before day one (manager's tasks)
- Submit the "New starter" request in the service desk portal at least 5 working days before the start date.
- Choose the laptop type: Standard or Developer.

## Day one
1. Collect the laptop from the IT desk on floor 2.
2. Sign in with the temporary password on your welcome card and set a new one.
3. Register for MFA with Northwind Authenticator.
4. Install the NorthLink VPN from the Company Portal.
5. Join the mandatory security awareness course within your first 7 days.

## Standard equipment
- 14-inch standard laptop, a headset, and a docking station if you work on site.
- Developer laptops are 16-inch with 32 GB of memory and need director approval.
