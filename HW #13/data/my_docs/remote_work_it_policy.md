# Remote Work IT Policy

- Work only on company laptops. Personal computers must not be used to access internal systems.
- Always connect to the NorthLink VPN before opening internal tools.
- Public Wi-Fi is allowed only with the VPN switched on.
- Lock your screen when you step away. The laptop locks automatically after 10 minutes.
- Do not let family members use your work laptop.
- Home printers must not be used for confidential documents.
- Staff working abroad for more than 14 days must tell IT beforehand, because some countries are blocked from our systems.
