# Phishing and Security Incident Reporting

## Suspicious email
1. Do not click links or open attachments.
2. Use the "Report phishing" button in Outlook. This sends it to the security team and removes it from your inbox.
3. If you already clicked a link or entered your password, change your password and call the service desk on extension 4357 immediately.

## Other security incidents
Report lost devices, unknown people in secure areas, or strange account activity to security@northwind.example. Reports are acknowledged within 1 hour during working hours.

## Training
Everyone completes the security awareness course within 7 days of joining and then once a year. Phishing tests are run every quarter.
