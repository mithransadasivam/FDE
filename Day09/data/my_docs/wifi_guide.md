# Wi-Fi Guide

## Networks
- **Northwind-Staff**: for company laptops. Connects automatically using your device certificate.
- **Northwind-Guest**: for visitors. Reception hands out a daily code that expires at midnight.
- Personal phones and tablets may only use Northwind-Guest.

## Connection problems
1. Turn Wi-Fi off and on again.
2. Choose "Forget this network" for Northwind-Staff and reconnect.
3. If it still fails, restart the laptop and try again before raising a ticket.

## Coverage
Wi-Fi is available on all office floors. The roof terrace and the car park have no coverage.
