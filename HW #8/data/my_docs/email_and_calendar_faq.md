# Email and Calendar FAQ

**How big can an email attachment be?** 25 MB. For larger files, share a NorthDrive link.

**How big is my mailbox?** 50 GB. You get a warning at 45 GB.

**How do I set an out-of-office reply?** In Outlook, choose Automatic Replies, set the dates and write your message. You can set a different message for people outside Northwind.

**How do I add a shared mailbox?** Ask the mailbox owner to raise a ticket granting you access. It appears in Outlook within one hour.

**Can I use my work email on my personal phone?** Yes, through the Outlook mobile app only. The phone must have a screen lock, and IT can wipe the work data if the phone is lost.
