# Service Desk Hours and Response Targets

## Contact
- Portal: servicedesk.northwind.example
- Phone: extension 4357 (external: 555-0143)
- Email: servicedesk@northwind.example

## Hours
Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 18:00. Outside these hours, only the emergency line (lost devices and security incidents) is staffed.

## Response targets
| Priority | Example | First response | Target fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 Critical | Company-wide outage | 15 minutes | 4 hours |
| P2 High | One team cannot work | 1 hour | 8 hours |
| P3 Normal | Single user issue | 4 hours | 3 working days |
| P4 Low | Request or question | 1 working day | 5 working days |

Tickets are closed after 5 working days without a reply from the requester.
