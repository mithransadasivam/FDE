# Password Policy

## Requirements
- At least 14 characters.
- Must not reuse any of your last 10 passwords.
- Passwords expire every 90 days. You get an email warning 14 days before.
- Five wrong attempts in a row lock the account for 30 minutes.

## Resetting a forgotten password
1. Go to the self-service page at reset.northwind.example.
2. Enter your work email and approve the MFA prompt.
3. Choose a new password that meets the rules above.

If you cannot pass the MFA step, contact the service desk. They will verify your identity with your manager before resetting.

## Password manager
Staff must store work passwords in the company password manager (VaultWorks). Do not keep passwords in spreadsheets or sticky notes.
