# ENG-602 · Send a field to a person when the pipeline is not sure, or the two methods disagree

**Type:** feature · **Priority:** Must · **Day 6, Activity 3** · **Story:** the analysts check only what needs checking

## Story

As Tom, a senior KYC analyst, I want us to check only the fields the pipeline might have wrong, so that we stop copy-typing every field of 8,600 documents a month and still make fewer mistakes than we do today.

## Acceptance criteria

1. `route_field(parser, vision, ok_at, use_agreement)` in `backend/app/pipeline/routing.py` returns a `Route` exactly as its docstring describes: `"auto"` with no reasons, or `"review"` with every reason, in order.
2. The reasons are, word for word: the field's note for a `missing` or `not_on_page` field (or `Not found on the page`); `OCR confidence 0.62 is below 0.85` for low confidence; `The vision model read "03 Oct 2026"` (or `The vision model found no value`) when the two methods disagree, compared on letters and digits only.
3. On **Pipeline → Routing**, with `extract_v2` checked against `extract_vlm_v1` at 0.85: confidence alone sends 6.8% of fields to a person and lets 20 errors through; adding agreement sends 10.9% and lets 3 through, and the result after the review beats one keyer at the bank.

## Files you change

- `backend/app/pipeline/routing.py`: fill in `route_field()`. Nothing else.

`norm()` is in the same file. The Routing tab, the Review queue and the Batch tab all call `route_field()` (done).

## Done when

- `cd backend && python -m pytest -q tests/test_routing.py` → 12 passed.
- `cd frontend && npx playwright test e2e/routing.spec.ts` → 3 passed.
- On **Review queue**, accept one field: the counter goes up by one, and the field moves to **Checked**.
