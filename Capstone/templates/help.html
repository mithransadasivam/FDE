How {{ brand.company_name }} works

A short guide for hiring managers, recruiters and auditors.

The flow

  1. Requisition — the hiring manager records a voice note; the platform writes the job and a fair rubric and checks the wording. The manager approves and publishes.
  2. Applications — candidates apply on the careers page and are told AI is used. CVs are read (PDF, Word, scans, screenshots); unsure fields wait in the review queue.
  3. Screening — every candidate is scored on every skill with the exact CV sentence behind it. The recruiter approves the shortlist.
  4. AI interview — invited candidates are interviewed by voice (they can ask for a person instead). Questions follow their answers; every answer is scored with a quote.
  5. Scorecard — the recruiter enters their own levels from the transcripts first, then hears the recording, and decides.
  6. Human interview and decision — the copilot follows a recruiter-led interview; the recruiter records the decision and an email waits for approval.

The twelve rules, in plain words

  • A person decides; the system only prepares and suggests. Every decision is logged with who, when and why.
  • Every score has evidence: a quote that code found in the CV or the transcript. No quote, no score.
  • Identity is hidden while scoring, and only content is scored, never tone, accent or voice.
  • Candidates are told and can ask for a person; asking never counts against them.
  • Core questions are the same for everyone; forbidden questions are flagged; text from candidates is treated as data, never instructions.
  • Each agent has least privilege; recordings are personal data and are deleted after the retention period; all data in demos is fictional; every claim is proven by a test or a report.

Questions

Why can't I see names? Hiring managers always see the blind view. Recruiters can switch identity on in the candidate list when they need to contact someone.

What does "scorers disagree: please review" mean? Two independent scorers rated the same skill two levels apart. The levels are never averaged: read the answer yourself.

How do I get my data out? The Analytics page, the CSV exports, the scorecard PDF and the audit bundle are all downloads. Contact: {{ brand.support_email }}.
