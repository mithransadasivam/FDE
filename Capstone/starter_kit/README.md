# Capstone starter kit (Day 1 material)

Everything here is fictional, made up for a training exercise: the people, their CVs, contact details
(example.com addresses, +44 7700 900xxx numbers), towns, companies and voice notes.

These files are NOT loaded into your app. They are test material: you upload them through the screens
your app provides, exactly as a hiring manager or a candidate would.

| Folder | What it is | Used in |
|---|---|---|
| CLAUDE_template.md | The project rules for Claude Code: copy it to CLAUDE.md in your project | Prompt 1 |
| voice_notes/ | Three hiring managers' voice notes (.wav), one per role, with the words spoken (.transcript.txt) so you can check your transcription, and so tests can run without a model | Prompts 5 to 9 |
| cvs/cloud_support_engineer/ | 52 CVs for the Cloud Support Engineer role: 20 PDF, 15 Word, 8 scanned (.jpg) and 9 profile screenshots (.png). index.csv lists them | Prompts 10 to 13 |
| answer_keys/extraction_answer_key.json | What a person reading 12 of the CVs (3 of each format) would extract | Prompt 11 check, and Day 2 evaluation |

Some CVs contain traps you will meet on later days. Do not remove them.
