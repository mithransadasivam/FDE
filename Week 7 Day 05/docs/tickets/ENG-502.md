# ENG-502 · The bank's rules, checked on the Terraform plan

**Type:** feature · **Priority:** Must · **Day 5, Activity 2** · **Story:** prove the pilot meets the rules before it exists

## Story

As the FDE, I want the bank's eight rules checked automatically on the pilot's Terraform plan, so that Helen sees, before anything is created, that the pilot keeps data in London, has no route to the internet, holds no secret in plain text, logs every model call and has a budget.

## Acceptance criteria

1. `check_pilot(plan)` in `backend/app/platform/pilot.py` returns one `PilotCheck` per rule in `RULES`, in that order, exactly as its docstring describes: `residency`, `model_in_region`, `no_internet`, `private_model`, `least_privilege`, `secrets`, `audit`, `spend`.
2. Each check has a one-line `evidence` naming what passes or breaks the rule, and the `resources` it looked at.
3. On **Pilot → Infrastructure**, the first plan (ADR-001's model, `eu.anthropic.claude-haiku-5-5`) shows 7 of 8 rules met: `model_in_region` fails, and the Bedrock box is red.

## Files you change

- `backend/app/platform/pilot.py`: fill in `check_pilot()`. Nothing else.

`resources()`, `of_type()`, `variable()`, `lambda_env()`, `statements()` and `as_list()` are in the same file (done). `residency()` is in `backend/app/platform/residency.py` (done).
