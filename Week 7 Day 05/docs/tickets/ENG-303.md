# ENG-303 · Field boxes on the document

**Type:** feature · **Priority:** Must · **Day 3, Activity 2 · session "ui"** · **Story:** S03

## Story

As an analyst, I want every field boxed on the page, coloured by how sure it is, so that I look at the doubtful part first instead of reading the whole document.

## Acceptance criteria

1. `FieldBoxes` in `frontend/src/components/FieldBoxes.tsx` draws, for every field with a `box`:
   - a `button` with `data-testid="box-<name>"`, `data-status="<status>"` and `data-selected="true|false"`
   - absolutely positioned at `left = x0`, `top = y0`, `width = x1 − x0`, `height = y1 − y0`, all as percentages of the page (the parent is a `relative` container the size of the image)
   - a coloured border from `STATUS_STYLE[status].border`, and a label chip from `chipText(field)` with `STATUS_STYLE[status].chip`: above the box, except for the address, whose chip goes below (it sits right under the name)
   - clicking a box calls `onSelect(name)`; the selected box stands out (for example a ring)
2. On **Cases → D03-014**, clicking a box highlights its field on the right, and clicking a field highlights its box.

## Files you change

- `frontend/src/components/FieldBoxes.tsx`: build the component. Nothing else.

`STATUS_STYLE` and `chipText()` are in `frontend/src/components/fieldStyle.ts` (done). The review screen that uses this component is `frontend/src/pages/cases/CaseReview.tsx` (done).
