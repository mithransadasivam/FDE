# Data Retention Policy

| Data type | How long we keep it |
|---|---|
| Support tickets | 3 years |
| System and security logs | 1 year |
| Email | 7 years |
| Chat messages | 90 days |
| Backups | 30 days (daily) / 12 months (monthly) |

## Rules
- Data past its retention date is deleted automatically each month.
- Legal hold: if the legal team places a hold, deletion is paused for the affected data.
- Do not keep copies of company data on personal devices or personal cloud storage.
