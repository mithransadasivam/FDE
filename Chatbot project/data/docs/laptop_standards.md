# Laptop Standards and Refresh

## Standard laptop
- 14-inch screen, 16 GB memory, 512 GB storage, Windows 11.
- Delivered with disk encryption, antivirus and the company VPN pre-installed.

## Refresh cycle
Laptops are replaced every 4 years. You will be contacted by IT about 2 months before your replacement.

## Software
- Only software from the Company Portal may be installed.
- Need something that is not listed? Raise a "Software request" ticket. Approval takes up to 3 working days.
- Local administrator rights are not given to standard users.

## Damage, loss and theft
Report a lost or stolen laptop to the service desk within 1 hour. Report accidental damage by raising a ticket; do not attempt repairs.
