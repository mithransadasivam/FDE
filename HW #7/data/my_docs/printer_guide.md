# Printer Guide

## Adding a printer
- Floor 1 printer name: PRN-FL1-COLOUR. Floor 2 printer name: PRN-FL2-MONO.
- Open Settings > Printers & scanners > Add device, search for the printer name, and click Add.

## Releasing a print job
Print jobs are held in a secure queue. Tap your staff badge on the printer to release them. Jobs not released within 24 hours are deleted.

## Common problems
- Paper jam: open the rear door and remove the paper gently, then close the door.
- Toner low: the facilities team replaces toner. Log a request in the portal; do not try to swap cartridges yourself.
- Colour printing is only available on the floor 1 printer. Colour jobs over 50 pages need manager approval.
