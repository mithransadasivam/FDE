# Video Meeting Rooms Guide

## Using a room
1. Book the room in Outlook; the room appears as a resource.
2. In the room, tap the touch panel and press "Join" to start your booked meeting.
3. To share your laptop screen, plug in the USB-C cable on the table or use the Share button in the meeting app.

## Problems
- No sound: check the table microphone is not muted (the light is green when live).
- Panel is blank: hold the power button on the panel for 5 seconds.
- Rooms on floor 3 use older equipment; use the HDMI cable instead of USB-C.

## Recording
Recording is allowed only if every participant is told at the start. Recordings are kept for 90 days.
