# Discovery summary: Kestrelmoor Bank, KYC Operations

*Source: the discovery session transcript (`data/Day01_Slide21_discovery_transcript.md`) and the client brief. Fictional client.*

## What we heard

- **Speed:** a 3-working-day promise; median is 7 working days, worst cases past 12 (Deborah). 4,200 documents waiting; the backlog has grown since March.
- **Volume:** about 1,900 applications a week, 2.4 documents each. January and September roughly double.
- **Business accounts:** about 15% of applications, about 40% of analyst time (Deborah).
- **Rework:** 31% of applications get at least one "ask applicant" (Tom). Usual reasons: proof of address older than 3 months, blurred passport photo, name mismatch. Resubmitted cases go back in the queue to a different analyst.
- **Time per document:** about 6 minutes personal, 20 or more for a company (Tom). Copy-typing into CaseFlow and two-screen switching are the main frustrations.
- **2024 pilot:** an OCR tool was dropped after 4 months because it never showed how sure it was.
- **Data:** about 18 months of history, roughly 61,000 document reviews, with reason codes.
- **Success:** Martin judges cost per application (about £11.40) and abandonment (22% give up when onboarding takes more than 5 days). Deborah: back inside 3 days for most customers before the January peak, and analysts who want to use the tool.
- **Rules (Helen):** a person decides; her sign-off for control changes; independent model validation (about 3 weeks); documents stay in the UK (AWS London); audit trail.

## Open questions

1. How may we use the review history, which contains customer data? (Helen)
2. What are the cost and abandonment targets for the pilot? (Martin)
3. Can Tom supply a sample of anonymised proof-of-address cases to test on? (Tom)
